  https://github.com/EVEngine/EVEngine/tree/dev/examples/character-motion-lab/assets/quaternius

Only the geometry, skin and node data were kept; materials and textures were
removed because the website renders the surface as a particle cloud. The
geometry, bind pose and skin weights are unchanged.
